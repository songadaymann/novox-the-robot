New Virtual Singer · UI directions

Four mockups of a 1000×640 plugin window. Each one shows all three ways to get a song in (MIDI + lyrics, audio in, voices), but leans on a different one. They're clickable, but there's no audio.

APiano roll + lyric lane

A DAW-style piano roll with each syllable written inside its note. Type in one lyric line and the syllables fill the notes in order, with a phoneme strip underneath.

BLyrics-first sheet

Write it like a lyric sheet. Small pitch marks float above each syllable, and when you capture MIDI, what you play fills the line one note at a time.

CAudio-drop workflow

Drop a sung take, analyze it, check the words and notes it heard, then accept them as notes. Words are colored by how sure the detection is, and you can A/B your take against the virtual singer.

DVoice designer (Aqua homage)

A playful early-2000s Mac look for picking and shaping voices: a gallery of face avatars, gel knobs, a vowel/formant chart, a choir-size crowd and a per-phoneme accent sheet.

Example lyric (original): “the ket-tle sings at sev-en, and I hum a-long”. The data is made up and the mockups have no real audio.
