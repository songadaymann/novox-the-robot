Novox The Robot is installed

  • App: open Novox The Robot from Applications.
  • Logic Pro / GarageBand: on a Software Instrument track, choose AU Instruments › Song A Day › Novox The Robot. Quit and reopen Logic if it was open.
  • Ableton Live and others: find Novox The Robot among your VST3 instruments (rescan plugins if it doesn't show up).
