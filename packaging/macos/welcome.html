Novox The Robot @LABEL@

Make your computer sing. Type lyrics, draw notes, or sing into it, and a robot sings it back.

This installs the standalone app in Applications, and the Audio Unit and VST3 plugins in /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins. Choose Customize on the next pages to leave any of them out.

It needs an Apple Silicon Mac with macOS @MIN_MACOS@ or later. The first time you import singing, Novox downloads its lyrics model (574 MB) with your permission.

Free and open source, by Jonathan Mann.
