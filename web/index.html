Piano Roll

Hover or tap a note to see its phonemes.

Lyrics

Words flow onto the notes. Longer words take one note per syllable. hel-lo splits a word, _ holds a vowel over the next note, and leftover notes are hummed.

Melody

Each note is PITCH BEATS, like C4 1 or F#4 0.5; r 1 is a rest. Separate with commas or new lines. You can also drop a .mid or .musicxml file anywhere on the window.
